Biography

About Jabillz

An independent artist and label founder, Jabillz is known for her soulful and heartfelt music, blending melody and emotion into songs that feel personal from the first listen.

She's the founder of Billion Vibe Records, where she writes, records, and shapes most of her sound herself. Since launching the label, she's released a steady run of singles and an EP, each one built around the same idea: music should make you feel something real.

Billion Vibe Records exists to give space to artists who lead with feeling first — a catalogue built on craft, not trend.

Beyond the Music — Introducing RelixZoon

Drawing from her personal experience navigating the challenges of the independent music industry, Jabillz is also the founder of RelixZoon, an artist-focused music discovery and fan-support platform.

Currently in development, RelixZoon is being built to give independent musicians greater control over their releases, audience relationships, and earnings. The platform brings together music discovery, direct fan support, exclusive releases, and opportunities for artists to monetize their creativity beyond traditional streaming.

Through Billion Vibe Records and RelixZoon, Jabillz continues to pursue a broader vision: creating music while building opportunities for independent artists to grow, connect with listeners, and earn more directly from their work.
